How selection works

Entries will be reviewed privately by The Drell Legacy Foundation. One recipient will be selected based on the information submitted. Social media likes, shares, comments, and follower counts will not determine the winner.

Enter the Giveaway

Parent/guardian nomination form

Please use parent or guardian contact information. Do not enter a child’s email address or phone number.

Required acknowledgments

Information submitted through this form will be used only to review giveaway entries, contact applicants, and administer this giveaway. The Drell Legacy Foundation will not publicly share a child’s personal information or nomination story without appropriate permission.

Official Giveaway Rules
  • No purchase or donation necessary.
  • Approximate retail value: $150.
  • One recipient will receive $150 in Foot Locker gift cards.
  • Parent or guardian participation is required for minors.
  • Only one entry per student.
  • Entries must be truthful and complete.
  • The Foundation may disqualify duplicate, fraudulent, or incomplete entries.
  • The recipient will be contacted using the parent/guardian information provided.
  • If the selected recipient cannot be reached within a reasonable period, the Foundation may select another recipient.
  • Gift cards are not redeemable for cash through the Foundation.
  • The giveaway is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Foot Locker, Facebook/Meta, or Instagram.
  • Participation does not create any tax-deductible donation or purchase requirement.
  • The Foundation reserves the right to modify or cancel the giveaway if circumstances require it.

About the Foundation

A legacy that lives through service.

The Drell Legacy Foundation was created to carry forward the generosity, leadership, compassion, and dedication to serving others embodied by Juandrell “Drell” Lewis.

We honor that spirit by creating meaningful opportunities to give, connect, and help our community thrive—one student, one family, and one act of service at a time.

DL

Our Mission

“To ensure that the generosity, leadership, and community spirit of Juandrell ‘Drell’ Lewis continues to inspire and uplift future generations.”

LegacyService with heart.
Purpose in action.

Drell’s Legacy

Turning remembrance into meaningful action.

Drell’s spirit of generosity and care is the heart of this Foundation. Every gathering, partnership, and initiative is intended to extend that spirit into the future.

We believe a legacy grows strongest when a community carries it together—showing up for young people, meeting neighbors with compassion, and making service a lasting tradition.
