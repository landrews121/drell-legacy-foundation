About the Foundation

A legacy that lives through service.

The Drell Legacy Foundation was created to carry forward the generosity, leadership, compassion, and dedication to serving others embodied by Juandrell “Drell” Lewis.

We honor that spirit by creating meaningful opportunities to give, connect, and help our community thrive—one student, one family, and one act of service at a time.

DL

Our Mission

“To ensure that the generosity, leadership, and community spirit of Juandrell ‘Drell’ Lewis continues to inspire and uplift future generations.”

LegacyService with heart.
Purpose in action.

Drell’s Legacy

Turning remembrance into meaningful action.

Drell’s spirit of generosity and care is the heart of this Foundation. Every gathering, partnership, and initiative is intended to extend that spirit into the future.

We believe a legacy grows strongest when a community carries it together—showing up for young people, meeting neighbors with compassion, and making service a lasting tradition.
