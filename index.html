Our Mission

To ensure that the generosity, leadership, and community spirit of Juandrell “Drell” Lewis continues to inspire and uplift future generations.

About the Foundation

A legacy built through service

The Drell Legacy Foundation was created to honor the life and impact of Juandrell “Drell” Lewis by continuing the work he cared deeply about. Through education, outreach, mentorship, and community service, we are committed to helping future generations thrive.
